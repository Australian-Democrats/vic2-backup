Why games?

Most people meet politics through headlines and attack ads — the worst possible teachers. A good game does what a pamphlet can't: it lets you sit in the chair, feel the trade-offs, and discover for yourself why integrity in politics is hard, and why it matters.

Every one of them runs in your browser on any device. If you enjoy them, share them with a mate — or a teenager who thinks politics is boring.
