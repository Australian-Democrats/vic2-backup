🎉 Welcome aboard

Thanks for joining the Australian Democrats!

Thank you for joining the Australian Democrats. We're thrilled to have you as part of our team. It's the support from members like you that will help us get elected and bring back accountability, integrity and trust to Australian politics.

🏛️

A note about your membership application

Your application will be formally considered by the National Executive of the Australian Democrats. In the meantime, you'll continue to receive party communications, updates and member material as though you are a member. If your application is unsuccessful, you'll be informed in writing and refunded the fee that accompanied your application.

How does membership work?

It's really simple.

✨

Free Supporter member

As a free supporter member you don't need to worry about renewing your membership every year. You're part of the team until you notify us otherwise.

💚

Paid member

As a paid member your membership renews automatically every year if you selected that option when you applied. If not, you'll get an email with renewal instructions in the lead-up to your membership anniversary. If you choose not to renew, your membership automatically switches to a free supporter membership when it expires — so you remain part of the team until you notify us otherwise.
