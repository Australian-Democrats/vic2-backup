How to vote

Find your how-to-vote card

Our registered how-to-vote cards for the 2026 Victorian state election.

You get two ballot papers at a Victorian state election — one for your district (the lower house) and one for your region (the upper house). Find our card for each below, then take it with you, print it, or send it to someone.

Victorian state election 2026

Our cards for your ballot papers

    We’ll show our card for your district and your region. You can also type a district or region.

    Not sure which district you’re in? Look up your address with the VEC (opens in a new tab)

    We’re not standing there at this election

    There’s no Australian Democrats how-to-vote card for that district or region.
