Our focus in Victoria

A practical voice for Victorians.

For decades the Australian Democrats have stood for sensible, community-focused politics and accountability in government. In Victoria, we are bringing that same approach to the issues that matter most to everyday people.

Too often the public conversation feels divided and dominated by political point-scoring rather than progress. We believe there is a better way — one focused on practical problem solving, listening to the community, and developing sensible policies that improve everyday life. Our goal is to bring a balanced, thoughtful voice back into Victorian politics and focus on solutions that genuinely work for people and communities.
