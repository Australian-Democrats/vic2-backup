Victorian state election

Back the Victorian campaign

Every dollar given here goes straight to the Australian Democrats Victoria State Campaign Account and is spent on the Victorian state election campaign.

Victoria's donation laws cap what one person can give a party during the election period at $5,190 but count donations of $100 or less outside that cap. They also require both the party and yourself to disclose donations of $1,290 or more. The form below keeps us inside those rules.

    Your disclosure obligation. If your donations to the Australian Democrats reach $1,290 in a financial year — in one donation or several — the Electoral Act 2002 (Vic) requires you, as well as the party, to disclose them to the Victorian Electoral Commission within 21 days, at VEC Disclosures. Donations of $100 or less do not count toward that total. We will send you a receipt for every donation.
