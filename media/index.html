Ground rules

Using the marks

Editorial use is welcome and needs no permission. Anything that could read as an endorsement does — just ask.

✓Please do

  • Use the supplied files at their original proportions.
  • Give the logo clear space on every side of at least the height of the “A”.
  • Pick the black or white mark by background, whichever holds contrast.
  • Refer to us as the Australian Democrats, or the Australian Democrats (Victoria).

✕Please don’t

  • Recolour, outline, add effects to or redraw the marks.
  • Stretch, squash, rotate or crop them.
  • Put the black mark on a dark background, or the white mark on a light one.
  • Use the logo in a way that implies we endorse a product, candidate or campaign.

Palette

Brand colours

Democrat Yellow is a highlighter, never a text colour. For coloured type on a light background use Deep Teal or darker so the contrast holds up in print and on screen.

Democrat Yellow #FCD666 Highlighter sweeps, primary buttons, accents. Never used as a text colour.

Teal Green #29A895 Rules, icons and large accents.

Deep Teal #147065 Links and small coloured type — the AA-safe teal.

Teal Ink #0D4A43 Hover states and deep accents.

Ink #1C2B29 Body text, borders and the footer block.

Sand #F5F0E2 Alternating section backgrounds.

Paper #FFFDF7 The page background. Not white — warm white.
