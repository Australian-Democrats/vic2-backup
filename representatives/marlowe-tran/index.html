What to put in your first message

  • Your name, and the address the problem is at
  • A phone number or email we can reach you on
  • What has already happened, with dates
  • Any reference number you have been given
  • What outcome you are after

What you send goes to Marlowe and their office. It is not added to a mailing list, and we do not pass it on without asking you first.

What a Member can — and can't — take up

Ours

  • Federal payments and services — Centrelink, Medicare, aged care, the NDIS
  • Passports, visas and citizenship
  • Bills before the House of Representatives, and how a member will vote on one
  • Federal funding promised to a project in the division

Someone else's — and who

  • Public hospitals, ambulance response, state schools and TAFE ↳ your state member of parliament
  • Rates, bins, footpaths, local roads and planning permits ↳ your council
  • A dispute with a bank, insurer or superannuation fund ↳ the Australian Financial Complaints Authority →

If you've already tried them and got nowhere, write to us anyway — we'll say so and point you on.

About Marlowe

This is a sample profile, published so the party can see exactly what a finished House of Representatives page looks like. Every element on it is editable in Keystatic.

Ballarat is a real federal division, but nobody of this name represents it and nothing on this page describes a real person. The office details, the current work and this paragraph are invented to show the layout.

The page deliberately carries no record of votes or motions and no committee memberships: under a real-looking name, invented ones read as claims about a real person, and the site refuses to publish them.

About Ballarat

Ballarat is a federal division in western Victoria. Like every division, it returns one member to the House of Representatives.
