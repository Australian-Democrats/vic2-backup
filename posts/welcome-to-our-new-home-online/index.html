Welcome to our new home online

The Victorian Australian Democrats' website is now fully party-built and party-owned — faster, more accessible, and editable by our own volunteers.

The Victorian division’s website now runs on infrastructure the party owns and controls, built in-house from the ground up.

What’s changed under the hood: every page loads faster, works properly on any device, meets modern accessibility standards, and can be updated by our own volunteers without touching code.

While we continue to work on improving the experience, some things may look out of place. If you experience any issues, please let us know.
